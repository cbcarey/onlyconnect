How to Play

Find groups of four books that share something in common: titles, authors, characters, forms, and more.

  • Pick four books, then tap Submit. A red bookmark marks each pick.
  • Get it right and the group is bound together with its category.
  • You can make four mistakes before the game ends.
  • If you’re one away, you’ll be told.
  • Some books could fit more than one group. Work out which fits where!
